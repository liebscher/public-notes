## Safron, frankincense, and sandalwood
April 30, 2026 6:00pm

| Before | During                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|        | - Earthy, musky, a little sour<br>- Herbal<br>- Warm<br>- Kind of old smelling, like an old book, car, or attic<br>- Woody oriental<br>- Leather<br>- Lavender<br>- Green flower stems<br>- Cedar, palo santo |

Takeaway: I think I don't really like the sourness in this incense. It kind of hurts my nose ever so slightly. Like I'm getting attacked. I like the warmth a lot though. I like how musky it is, and the "old" aromatics I'm getting. The lavender might also not be great for me. I love the woody-ness of this incense. In the past I haven't really liked palo santo, it smelled like sap burning and was really way too strong. Just thinking about woody notes. I love smelling freshly cut wood. I used to do some woodworking and part of the reason I liked it was for the smell of a woodshop or lumber yard. This one is from a pack I got at the market.
