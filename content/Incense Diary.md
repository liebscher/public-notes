## Safron, frankincense, and sandalwood
- Earthy, musky, a little sour
- Herbal
- Warm
- Kind of old smelling, like an old book, car, or attic
- Woody oriental
- Leather
- Lavender
- Green flower stems
- Cedar, palo santo

I think I don't really like the sourness in this incense. It kind of hurts my nose ever so slightly. Like I'm getting attacked. I like the warmth a lot though. I like how musky it is, and the "old" aromatics I'm getting. The lavender might also not be great for me. I love the woody-ness of this incense. In the past I haven't really liked palo santo, it smelled like sap burning and was really way too strong. Just thinking about woody notes. I love smelling freshly cut wood. I used to do some woodworking and part of the reason I liked it was for the smell of a woodshop or lumber yard. This one is from a pack I got at the market.

## Blend of cooling aqua notes with the warmth of oud

Before burning:
- musky, very masculine, a bit sharp like a cologne
- Almost floral, like roses
- Kind of woody but not too much

Once burning:
- Fairly soft, mild, woody
- Like a darker wood, not a bright wood. I'm imagining like redwood vs pine
- Fresh, almost like clean laundry, or detergent
- Sort of coastal
- Something soapy
- A little green, like leaves
- Maybe kind of like amber (uncertain)

I like how mild and soft this one is. It's very fresh smelling, it's sort of on the borderline between masculine and feminine. It's fresh, not too sweet or floral, and not sour/sharp. This one is from a pack from Jess.

## Patchouli
- Warm, soft
- Reminds me a lot of fresh laundry, like if laundry were warm or firey
- Herbal, in a church-like way, like frankencense perhaps
- A bit sharp, but pleasant, reminds me of a cigarette in that way
- An ancient or traditional smell
- Not flowery or sweet, citrusy or aquatic
- A bit like leather or musk
- A bit like a light wood burning
- Like leaves burning in a campfire
- A bit of a unisex scent, although leaning masculine
- On the drier side as opposed to resinous and syrupy

I like it, it's calming and probably good to meditate with. I would light this if I wanted a calm evening in, maybe listening to some earthy, folksy, religious, or "Eastern" music. This is the Patchouli from my Auromere sampler.
