Years ago I started a blog. I contributed a variety of tutorials, essays, opinion pieces, and article reviews on there. But there was always a fundamental mismatch between the quantity of shareable thoughts, ideas, and projects I had, and the frequency of publishing.

The chasm from private to public was made too large by the standards I held myself to for what it meant to be "good enough."

Recently, I thought maybe I was simply using infrastructure as an excuse to procrastinate. I decided, what if I wrote privately with the intention that one day I would figure out how to publish. Would I actually write?

The answer was Yes. I did.

I also looked around the web at sites that showcased a person's writing and found myself drawn to what feel like polar opposites: digital gardens of notes, and long-form essays with real thought and care. This tension created pull but also resistance. I want to share half-baked ideas but also well-research work. A paragraph and a mini-series.

This new site will hopefully alleviate this tension: enabling me to share thoughts without feeling trapped under the ice of a framework or a set of standards.

The ultimate goal of my writing is to clarify and develop my thoughts, and give interesting people a reason to start a conversation with me. Metaphorically, this is me [working with the garage door open](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/Work_with_the_garage_door_up). If you're reading this, let me know what you find interesting.
